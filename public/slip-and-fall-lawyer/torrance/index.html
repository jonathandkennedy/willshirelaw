Straight Answers

Questions Everyone Asks

Do I really have a case just from falling?

If you fell because of a hazard the owner knew about or should have found — a spill, a broken step, poor lighting, an uneven walkway, ice, debris — and you were hurt, yes. The question is what they knew and when. That's what the footage and maintenance logs show, and it's why we act fast.

I didn't report it to the store when it happened.

You can still have a case. Go get medical care, write down everything you remember, keep the shoes and clothes you were wearing, and call us. We'll figure out how to document it.

The store's insurer offered to pay my medical bills.

Don't sign anything. That offer almost always comes with a release of every claim you have — including the pain, lost work, and future care they're not mentioning. Get a free review first.

How long do I have to file?

In California you generally have two years from the accident to file a lawsuit (Code of Civil Procedure §335.1) — and if a city, county, or state agency is involved (a bus, a public road, a government vehicle) you may have as little as six months to file a claim. Evidence disappears far sooner: camera footage gets erased and witnesses move. The sooner we start, the stronger your case.

I fell on city property. Is that different?

Yes — and urgently. Claims against a city, county, or state agency generally must be filed within six months. If you fell on a public sidewalk, in a park, or at a government building, call today.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front — ever. We front every cost of building your case and only collect a fee if we recover money for you. No recovery, no attorney fee.
