Straight Answers

Questions Everyone Asks

What does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front — ever. The consultation is free, we front every cost of building your case, and we only collect an attorney fee if we recover money for you. No recovery, no attorney fee.

The insurance company already offered me money. Should I take it?

Not before a free case review. First offers are low by design — adjusters are trained to settle before you know what your claim is worth. Signing usually ends your claim permanently, even if your injuries get worse.

The other driver was uninsured. Do I still have a case?

Often, yes. Your own uninsured/underinsured motorist coverage may pay — and your own insurer will fight you just as hard as the other side's would. We handle UM/UIM claims every day.

How long do I have to file?

In California you generally have two years from the accident to file a lawsuit (Code of Civil Procedure §335.1) — and if a city, county, or state agency is involved (a bus, a public road, a government vehicle) you may have as little as six months to file a claim. Evidence disappears far sooner: camera footage gets erased and witnesses move. The sooner we start, the stronger your case.

I don't have health insurance. Can I still get treated?

Yes. We help clients get the care they need — often on a lien, so providers are paid from the settlement instead of out of your pocket. Don't skip treatment; it hurts your health and your case.

What if I was partly at fault?

You can still recover. California uses pure comparative negligence — your recovery is reduced by your share of fault, even if it's more than half. Don't accept the insurer's version of who's to blame.
